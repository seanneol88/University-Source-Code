Kopi Pantai Café

Welcome to Kopi Pantai Café, a cosy seaside café located just five minutes from campus. We serve authentic Terengganu breakfast and freshly brewed local coffee at student-friendly prices.

How to Order

  1. Choose your food and drinks from the menu board.
  2. Place your order at the counter, or scan the QR code on your table.
  3. Pay by cash or e-wallet and collect your order number.
  4. Relax — we will call your number when your order is ready.
