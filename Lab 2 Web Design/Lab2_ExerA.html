About the Event

FSKM Tech Day 2026 is a one-day event organised by the Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics. Join us for talks, hands-on sessions and a mini hackathon. Open to all students, free of charge.

Speaker: Dr. Siti Mariam

Senior data scientist who will share how machine learning is used in real Malaysian industries.

Activity: Mini Hackathon

Teams of three solve a fun coding challenge in three hours. Prizes for the top three teams!
